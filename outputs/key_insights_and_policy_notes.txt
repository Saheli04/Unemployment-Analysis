UNEMPLOYMENT ANALYSIS: KEY INSIGHTS
==================================================

1. The average observed unemployment rate was 8.26 percentage points higher during March-June 2020 than before March 2020.

2. Tripura had the highest mean observed unemployment rate among regions in this dataset (28.35%).

3. Meghalaya had the lowest mean observed unemployment rate among regions in this dataset (4.80%).

4. The urban observations had the higher mean unemployment rate in the rural/urban comparison (13.17%).

5. Policy consideration: examine region-specific employment support rather than relying only on a single national average.

6. Policy consideration: assess rural and urban workers separately when designing job placement, training and income-support programmes.

7. Policy consideration: track unemployment alongside labour participation and employment estimates.

8. Limitation: these comparisons show associations over time, not proof that COVID-19 alone caused every observed change.

9. Limitation: the dataset ends in June 2020 and cannot measure the full long-term recovery.

10. Limitation: monthly averages here are unweighted averages of the available region/area observations, not official national rates.

11. Limitation: roughly 14 months of data are insufficient to establish reliable recurring annual seasonality.
